Privacy Policy & Data Protection Notice

Last updated: 10 November 2026. This notice is issued under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) of India.

1. Who we are

ProSync Infotech ("we", "us") organises the Grand E-Com AI Summit Mumbai 2027 (16 January 2027, Radisson Hotel Mumbai Andheri MIDC). We are the Data Fiduciary for the personal data collected on this website.

3. Consent

We process your data only on the basis of your consent, given by ticking the consent box on the registration form. Consent is free, specific, informed and unambiguous. You may withdraw it at any time as easily as you gave it, by emailing support@prosyncedu.com. Withdrawal does not affect processing done before it, and may mean we cannot continue your registration.

4. Who we share data with

  • Payment gateway: your name, email and phone are passed to our payment provider so you can pay.
  • CRM / form-handling and communication tools used by us to store registrations and contact you.
  • Venue and event partners, only to the extent needed to run the event.
  • Google: this site loads Google Fonts and an embedded Google Map, which may receive your IP address and browser details.

We do not sell your personal data. Where required by law, we may disclose data to government or law-enforcement authorities.

5. Retention

We keep registration data until the event concludes and for up to 12 months afterwards for support, accounting and legal compliance, unless you ask us to erase it earlier (subject to legal retention duties). After that it is erased or anonymised.

6. Security

We use reasonable safeguards such as encrypted transmission (HTTPS) and restricted access to protect your data. In the event of a personal data breach, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as required by law.

7. Your rights under the DPDP Act

  • Access a summary of your personal data and how it is processed.
  • Correct or update inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erase your data when it is no longer needed for its purpose.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.
  • Grievance redressal, and to nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity.

To exercise any right, email support@prosyncedu.com or call +91 88511 29566. We aim to respond within 30 days.
